# Donations

OutyDS is free and open-source software distributed under GNU GPL-3.0. Donations are voluntary and help support continued development. Support is provided on a best-effort basis, without guaranteed response times, fixes, updates or compatibility.

## Where
The application links to two donation pages (the same two links are in `app/assets/app.js`):

- Revolut: https://revolut.me/stefancangri
- Buy Me a Coffee: https://buymeacoffee.com/cangri

## What a donation does and does not do
- Donations are **voluntary**.
- The application **remains free**, with or without a donation.
- Donations **do not unlock features**.
- Donations **do not grant priority support**.
- **No service or support contract is created** by donating.

Donations are handled by the payment providers above, under their own terms. OutyDS does not process payments.
